Verify degrees without calling the university

Universities record a fingerprint of every degree, transcript and certificate on a shared consortium blockchain. Employers and embassies scan the QR code on the document and get an answer in seconds.

How it works

1The university fills in the credential. The server builds a JSON record with a random salt and computes its SHA-256 fingerprint.
2Only the fingerprint goes on-chain (CredentialRegistry.issue). The full record is encrypted with AES-256-GCM; the vault stores ciphertext only.
3The decryption key is placed in the QR code on the PDF. Whoever holds the document can open the record; nobody else can.
4The verifier's browser decrypts the record, recomputes the fingerprint and asks the chain whether it matches and is still valid.
